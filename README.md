# Fitness-Tracker-Project-Group12

# Why (Question/Topic being investigated)

Many FitTrack users set daily calorie-burn or activity goals, but often fail to meet them because they only assess progress late in
the day, making it too late to take corrective action. This lack of timely feedback has been linked to decreased motivation and
long-term user churn.
To address this issue, a goal-setting strategy can be implemented that utilizes early-day activity and health data, such as steps
taken, sleep quality, heart rate metrics, and past activity trends. By analyzing this data, users can predict their end-of-day total
calorie burn and determine whether they are on track or at risk of missing their goal. This enables timely, actionable guidance
such as recommending a short walk before the day ends.
